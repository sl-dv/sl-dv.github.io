## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [TU Wien](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/) working with [Stefan Neumann](https://neumannstefan.com).

My research is on sublinear and streaming algorithms, with a focus on subgraph counting problems and their applications.

Previously, I earned my Master's degrees in Mathematics and Computer Science from the [University of Bonn](https://www.mathematics.uni-bonn.de/en).
